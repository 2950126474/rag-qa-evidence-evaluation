# Knowledge-base QA evaluation — detailed evidence report

## 1. Batch contract

Summarize objective, platforms, versions, corpus condition, question-set version, configuration snapshot, sessions, cache/retry policy, scoring, boundaries, and deviations. Link the batch manifest.

## 2. Executive result index

| Question | Platform | Verdict | Key facts | Citation | Failure layer | Evidence unit |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

This table indexes evidence; it does not replace the per-question records.

## 3. Per-question evidence

For every question include:

1. exact question and diagnostic purpose;
2. reviewed gold answer, atomic facts, and source anchor;
3. source-page screenshot;
4. each platform's raw first answer and session link;
5. answer screenshots required by the declared screenshot policy;
6. each platform's retrieval, citation, chunk, or knowledge-point evidence, with screenshots only when required by the policy or needed for diagnosis;
7. key-fact and unreserved verdicts;
8. citation verdict;
9. narrowest supported failure layer;
10. evidence gaps and acquisition instructions for required but unavailable evidence;
11. permitted and prohibited conclusions.

## 4. Cross-question analysis

Separate answer accuracy, citation quality, retrieval reproducibility, parsing/chunk behavior, stability, latency, and any other declared dimensions. Do not collapse them into one unexplained winner score.

## 5. Exceptions and limitations

List non-comparable conditions, retries, cache effects, hidden platform settings, missing screenshots, pending gold answers, and excluded questions.

## 6. Claims and next actions

For each proposed conclusion, link the supporting evidence units. Separate confirmed findings, provisional findings, and untested hypotheses.
